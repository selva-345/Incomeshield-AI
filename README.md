# IncomeShield AI  
Real-Time Income Protection Platform for Gig Workers  
 

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Phase%202-orange)

---

## Overview

The gig economy has become a major part of India’s workforce, especially for delivery partners working in food, grocery, and e-commerce platforms.

These workers depend on daily activity to earn. However, their income is highly affected by real-world conditions such as heavy rain, extreme heat, pollution, and traffic disruptions.

Even a few hours of disruption can reduce their daily earnings significantly. At present, there is no simple system that supports them during such income loss.

IncomeShield AI is built to solve this problem by providing a real-time income protection system that works automatically and requires no manual claims.

---

## Problem Statement

Gig workers face unpredictable income due to external factors beyond their control.

Current insurance solutions do not cover income loss caused by environmental or real-world disruptions. Most systems are slow, require manual claims, and are not designed for daily earners.

We aim to build a system that:
- Detects disruptions automatically  
- Provides instant support  
- Requires zero manual effort  
- Is simple and accessible  

---

## Solution

IncomeShield AI is a parametric income protection platform.

Instead of waiting for users to file claims, the system continuously monitors external data such as weather, air quality, and traffic conditions.

When a disruption crosses a predefined threshold, the system automatically triggers a payout.

### Key Features

- Real-time disruption monitoring  
- Automatic claim triggering  
- Weekly pricing model  
- Smart premium adjustment based on risk  
- Transparent and fast payouts  

---

## How It Works

1. User registers and selects their city  
2. System calculates a risk profile  
3. Weekly premium is generated based on risk  
4. External conditions are monitored continuously  
5. Disruption is detected  
6. Claim is automatically triggered  
7. Payout is processed instantly  

---

## Smart Premium System

The platform adjusts premiums dynamically:

- Low risk → Lower premium  
- High risk → Higher premium  

This ensures fair pricing for gig workers and makes the system practical for real-world usage.

---

## Parametric Triggers

The system monitors:

- Heavy rainfall  
- High pollution levels (AQI)  
- Extreme temperatures  
- Traffic disruptions  

Each trigger has a predefined threshold. Once crossed, payout is automatically activated.

---

## Fraud Prevention

To ensure reliability, the system includes:

- Location validation  
- Behavior tracking  
- Pattern detection  
- Multi-source verification  

This helps prevent false claims while protecting genuine users.
